
EXPERIMENT 3: MULTI-TASK LANE DETECTION AND ROAD SEGMENTATION

Dataset:
- Total images: 5,000
- Image resolution: 1248 x 384
- Training split: 3,500 images (000001-003500)
- Validation split: 750 images (003501-004250)
- Test split: 750 images (004251-005000)

Model:
- Architecture: Multi-task U-Net
- Shared encoder with task-specific lane and road decoders
- Training epochs: 30
- Best validation epoch: 27
- Best validation loss: 0.0326142173
- Device: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 Laptop GPU

Visual inspection:
- Predictions generally follow lane markings and road boundaries.
- Lane detection has more visible errors than road segmentation.
- Crosswalk treatment follows the dataset's existing mask conventions.

Limitations:
- Evaluation was performed on the held-out test split from the same
  CARLA data collection setup.
- Performance on other towns, weather conditions, lighting conditions,
  or real-world roads has not been established.
- The dataset consists of sequential frames, so temporal correlation
  between nearby frames may affect how broadly the results generalize.
